# Module Overview

> **Project status:** foundation / scaffold only. **All 18 modules below are Planned.**
> Only the project foundation / scaffold is present. No DocTypes or business logic exist.

Vidyqor is organized into 18 functional modules. Each has a `snake_case` folder under
`vidyqor/` with a `doctype/` placeholder today. The table lists every module, its folder,
status, and a short description of its intended scope.

| # | Module | Folder | Status | Description (planned) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Institution Management | `institution_management` | Planned | Institution profiles, campuses, academic calendar, and global settings. |
| 2 | Student Management | `student_management` | Planned | Student records, profiles, enrollment, and lifecycle. |
| 3 | Admission Management | `admission_management` | Planned | Applications, entrance workflows, offers, and onboarding. |
| 4 | Academic Management | `academic_management` | Planned | Programs, courses, terms, timetables, and grading. |
| 5 | Department Management | `department_management` | Planned | Departments, their structure, and resource ownership. |
| 6 | Faculty Management | `faculty_management` | Planned | Faculty profiles, assignments, and workload. |
| 7 | Attendance Management | `attendance_management` | Planned | Attendance capture, policies, and reporting. |
| 8 | Examination Management | `examination_management` | Planned | Exam scheduling, marks entry, results, and transcripts. |
| 9 | Finance And Fee Management | `finance_and_fee_management` | Planned | Fee structures, invoicing, payments, and ledgers. |
| 10 | Library Management | `library_management` | Planned | Catalog, circulation, memberships, and fines. |
| 11 | Hostel Management | `hostel_management` | Planned | Room allocation, occupancy, and hostel fees. |
| 12 | Transport Management | `transport_management` | Planned | Routes, vehicles, assignments, and transport fees. |
| 13 | Placement Management | `placement_management` | Planned | Recruiters, drives, applications, and offers. |
| 14 | Human Resource Management | `human_resource_management` | Planned | Staff records, leave, and HR processes. |
| 15 | Communication Management | `communication_management` | Planned | Notifications, announcements, and messaging. |
| 16 | Document Management | `document_management` | Planned | Document storage, templates, and issuance. |
| 17 | Reports And Analytics | `reports_and_analytics` | Planned | Dashboards, reports, and institution-wide analytics. |
| 18 | System Configuration | `system_configuration` | Planned | Roles, permissions, master data, and app settings. |
